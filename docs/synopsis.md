# Synopsis - Devil Children: Puzzle de Call! (GBA, 2002)

A puzzle spin-off of Devil Children Light Book / Dark Book (English: DemiKids Light/Dark Version). Built from a read of
the extracted script (`script/story.json`, `work/story_dump.txt`); names per glossary.md.

## Cast
- **Jin** (Light hero, human DemiKid) and his partner demon **Rand** (a Sol Cat, furry, uses ボク, cautious).
- **Akira** (Dark hero) and his partner demon **Gale** (a Rox, gruff and proud, tsundere toward the "kids").
- **Azrael**: the Emperor of the Imperium's younger brother; created the miniature world and controls it. Never seen
  in person: the Imperium bosses here, Azrael included, are **astral bodies**; their real bodies stay in Valhalla.
- Imperium bosses (one per area, boss.json): Crosel, Ozemos, Grimlee (female), Bifronz, Kyme, then Azrael.
- Helper demons: **Baku** (dying, katakana speech, script 1/21-23), **Laksmi** (rescued by Rand, later tends Baku
  and finds Gale and Akira), **Osiris** (dignified, "フム", explains the despair of the captives), **Mini Icy**
  (a Frost, "-ho" tic, knows the Imperium's officers).
- Tutorial host: Rand. Rescue lines: the demons freed from eggs on each stage (one or two lines each).

## Story (story.json, 29 scripts; scripts 0-14 = Rand's route, 15-28 = Gale's route)
0. Prologue (both routes): the four sense strong magic near an empty village on the edge of Valhalla. In a house
   they find a miniature of Valhalla. Jin and Akira touch it and are sucked in; Rand and Gale jump in after them.
   **Rand's route (1-14).** You play Rand; Jin is found along the way.
1. Rand wakes alone in a world inside the miniature. A dying Baku says the demons brought here have their magic
   drained to hold the world up. Rand resolves to regroup and escape.
2-4. Spring -> forest -> grassland. After the first boss Rand frees Laksmi: the boss was an Imperium corps commander;
   captured demons were all taken from Valhalla to have their magic drained. Free any you find.
5. A village: Rand finds Jin. Jin's info: an Imperium officer created and controls this world; learning about him
   may be the key to escaping.
6-8. The volcano; Rand wonders why only they were sucked in ("someone called us?"). The Sea of Trees.
9. Osiris: captives tried to escape and gave up; a few kept calling to Valhalla for help -> the heroes were
   summoned. An officer who knows the creator was taken to the dungeon on the Fjorgyn Snowfield.
10-11. Snowfield; Mini Icy: the creator is **Azrael**, the Emperor's younger brother; the bosses are astral bodies,
   so beating them doesn't defeat the real ones. The fix: take **control** of the world from Azrael, at **Quadriga**.
12-13. Quadriga; Azrael beaten; control of the world is theirs; the captives can go home.
14. Ending: back in Valhalla with Akira and Gale; banter (Rand boasts, Gale sulks); Akira suspects the Emperor has
   other plans; they decide to destroy the miniature somewhere hidden. Narration, then a centred epilogue, おわり.
   **Gale's route (15-28).** You play Gale, looking for Akira.
15-20. Gale alone; frees Osiris (the Imperium needs huge magic for some big plan); glimpses Akira sneaking off.
21. Gale finds Akira, who has learned the truth: the world is a **magic reservoir**; surplus magic is stored to keep
   Valhalla stable in a crisis. Good aim, wrong method. Baku screams from the spring.
22-25. They save Baku; Laksmi tends him, then explains: Azrael controls the world; destroying it would kill everyone
   inside; defeat Azrael at Quadriga to take control.
26-27. Quadriga; Azrael beaten.
28. Ending: reunion with Jin and Rand; Akira reflects that the Imperium's wish to improve Valhalla was sincere even if
   its method was wrong; Jin runs off eager for more adventure. Narration, centred epilogue, おわり.

## Tone
Kids' adventure: light, quick, jokey banter between the partners; the villains are pompous; the captive demons are
weak and grateful, some funny (katakana broken speech, Frost "-ho"). No reveal to protect except Azrael being the
creator and the astral-body twist (script 11/25): earlier lines must not name Azrael.
